# VVU Campus Safety — User manual

For students, staff and security officers using the Android app.
The admin dashboard has its own guide (ADMIN_MANUAL.md).

## Part A — Students and staff

### 1. Getting started

**Create your account**

1. Open the app and tap **Create an account**.
2. Enter your full name exactly as on your VVU ID card, your VVU ID number
   (for example `223IT0100001`), your phone number, email and a password of
   at least 8 characters.
3. Tap **Create account**. The app checks your ID against the university's
   records.
   - "ID not found" or "This ID is not active": contact the Security Office.
   - "The name you entered doesn't match": check the spelling of your surname.
   - "This ID is already registered": someone has used your ID. Contact the
     Security Office straight away.
4. Confirm your email with the link we send you. A yellow banner reminds you
   until you do; tap **Resend** if the email didn't arrive.

**Allow location and notifications**

The first time you sign in, the app explains two permissions. Allow both:

- **Location** lets security see where you are, *only while an SOS is
  active*.
- **Notifications** tell you when an officer is on the way, and bring
  campus safety announcements.

If you decline, the app still works, but the home screen shows a warning
card with a button to fix it. Without location, security will have to ask
where you are.

**Forgot your password?** On the log-in screen tap **Forgot password?**,
enter your email and tap **Send reset link**.

### 2. In an emergency: SOS

1. Press the big red **SOS** button.
2. A red screen counts down (3 seconds unless the Security Office changed
   it). It sends by itself when the count ends.
   - Pressed by mistake? Tap **Cancel**.
   - No time to wait? Tap **Send now**.
3. The screen changes to **SOS active**. It shows:
   - **Security has been alerted**: every on-duty officer has been notified.
   - **<Officer> is on the way**: an officer has accepted your alert.
   - **<Officer> has arrived**: the officer is at your location.
4. While the SOS is active, your location is shared with security and
   updates as you move. Keep the app open if you can.
5. Use **Call security** to speak to the Security Office, or **Chat with
   security** to write to the officer.
6. If you are safe before an officer accepts, press and hold **Hold if
   you're safe** until the circle fills. Once an officer has accepted, tell
   them by phone or chat instead.

**Emergency contacts get a text.** If you have added emergency contacts
(see §5), each one receives a text with your name and a Google Maps link
to your location.

**No mobile data?** The app shows **No connection yet** with two buttons:
**Call security** and **Text security my location** (an SMS with a map
link). Your alert also sends by itself as soon as the phone reconnects.

### 3. Reporting a non-urgent incident

Use this for things that have already happened, such as a theft, or
something suspicious.

1. On the home screen tap **Report an incident**.
2. Choose what happened (for example Theft) and describe it: what, when,
   and anyone involved (at least 10 characters).
3. Optional: add up to 3 photos with **Camera** or **Gallery**.
4. Check the pin on the map. Tap the map to move the pin if it happened
   somewhere else.
5. Tap **Send report**. Security sees your name and phone number with the
   report.

Photos need a connection. Without one, the app tells you; a report without
photos is saved and sent automatically when you reconnect ("Waiting to
send").

### 4. Following up

- **My reports** lists everything you have raised (reports and SOS
  alerts), newest first, with its status.
- Open one to see its **Updates**: when it was received, accepted, attended
  and closed. Tap **Chat with security** to message the officers about it.
  You can send a photo in the chat too.
- You get a notification when the status changes or an officer replies.

You can only see your own reports. Nobody else on campus can see them
except the security team.

### 5. Emergency contacts

1. On the home screen tap **Emergency contacts**.
2. Tap **Add contact**, enter the name, phone number and (optionally) the
   relationship, and tap **Save**.
3. Tap a contact to edit it, or the bin icon to remove it.

You can have up to 5 contacts. Only you can see them.

### 6. Announcements

Tap the megaphone icon at the top of the home screen to read safety
notices from the Security Office. The newest one also shows on the home
screen.

## Part B — Security officers

Officer accounts are created by the Security Office; you can't sign up
yourself. Sign in with the email and temporary password you were given,
then change the password with **Forgot password?**.

### 1. Going on duty

On your home screen, switch **On duty** on at the start of your shift and
off at the end. Only on-duty officers receive alert notifications. The
list below the switch shows every open alert: SOS first, then the oldest.

### 2. Responding to an alert

A new SOS sounds a loud 10-second siren and vibrates strongly, using your
phone's alarm volume, so keep that turned up. A new report sounds a normal
notification. Tap the notification, or the alert in your list, to open it.
You see:

- the reporter's position on a map (it moves while their SOS is active);
- their name, phone number, the nearest campus area and the time raised;
- the description and photos, for reports;
- the **Timeline** of everything that has happened.

Use the buttons in order:

| Button | When |
| --- | --- |
| **Accept** | You are taking the alert. The reporter sees that you're on the way. |
| **Go** | Opens Google Maps directions to the reporter. |
| **Call** / **Chat** | Contact the reporter. |
| **I'm on scene** | You have reached the reporter. |
| **Resolve** | The situation is handled. Add a short note on what was done. |
| **Mark as false alarm** | It was not a real emergency. Add why. |
| **Assign** | Add a colleague; they can then also mark on scene and resolve. |
| **Add note** | Record anything you saw or did; it goes on the timeline. |

Only officers assigned to an alert (by accepting it or being assigned) can
mark it on scene or resolve it. Any officer can mark a false alarm.

### 3. Announcements

Tap the megaphone icon to read notices for officers and for everyone.
